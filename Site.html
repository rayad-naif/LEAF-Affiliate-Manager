Documentation & Support

1. Getting Started

Install the application from the marketplace. Once authorized, our system will begin scanning for available campaigns.

2. Configuring Products

Visit the "Products & Rules" tab. Here you map product IDs to specific payout values. Choose between 'CASH' or 'LEAD' types to define rewards.

3. Syncing Subscriptions

Click "Sync Subscriptions" to force an immediate update. The system will create transaction records for every identified subscription.

4. Troubleshooting

If leads are not appearing, ensure the Affiliate ID matches the mapping in our database. Contact support at support@irofficial.com.
